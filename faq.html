Frequently asked questions

Helpful answers before we begin.

Every project is different. These answers cover the basics and the contact form is the best place to describe your specific opportunity.

What types of opportunities do you support?

Support can include government RFPs and RFQs, grant applications, tender responses, capability statements, and business proposals.

Can you work with a short deadline?

Availability depends on the opportunity scope, readiness of source materials, and current schedule. Share the deadline early so it can be assessed realistically.

Do you guarantee a contract award or grant?

No. A decision depends on many factors outside the proposal. The focus is to strengthen the quality, clarity, compliance, and strategic positioning of your submission.

What do you need to get started?

The solicitation or brief, deadline, relevant organization information, and any existing drafts or supporting materials are ideal starting points.

Will my information remain confidential?

Project information should be handled carefully. Do not send sensitive material through an unprotected form; confidentiality arrangements can be discussed before sharing detailed files.

How is project pricing determined?

Pricing is scoped based on document type, opportunity complexity, inputs, turnaround time, and the level of strategic or management support required.
